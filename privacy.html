Privacy: CORE public prototype

Updated 21 October 2026 (V9.9). This page describes what the public prototype actually does. It is not legal advice and claims no compliance (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or other): no legal professional has reviewed it.

What CORE does not do

  • No account, no sign-in and no form sent to a CORE server: CORE has no application server or database for visitors.
  • No cookies set by CORE, no analytics, no advertising trackers.
  • CORE reads answers aloud only when you press Read answer; CORE never records or keeps audio.
  • CORE does not train any model on what you type.

Storage in your browser

Everything you create stays in this browser under core.* keys (localStorage and sessionStorage): your language, avatar and pseudonym, followed missions, contributions, the local state of the agents and your ideas (the idea vault is encrypted with your passphrase). The “My CORE” page (TRUST section) lists these items and erases them; clearing your browser data also removes them. If you enable on-device AI, the model files are kept in your browser’s cache until you clear it.

Hosting and third parties

The site is static, served by Vercel, Inc. As with any website, Vercel processes your IP address and request details to deliver pages, under its own terms. Fonts and the map library are served by CORE itself. Your browser contacts these services directly:

ServiceWhyWhen
OpenFreeMapMap tiles and labels (© OpenStreetMap contributors)When the map is shown
NASA GIBSSatellite imagery at planet scaleWhen the map is shown
AWS Terrain TilesRelief“High” quality only
Photon (komoot)Finding unindexed places; receives the requested place name, map context center and IP address. Consent or refusal is remembered in this browser under core.geocode.consent; search history is not savedOnly if you switch this option on
Hugging Face, GitHubOne-time download of the on-device AI model files (they see your IP address and the files requested, never your text)Only if you enable on-device AI

The page’s content-security policy restricts CORE requests; it does not control the browser provider’s speech service. On the founder’s own computer only, the site can also use a local model; that address is never contacted from the public site.

CORE Intelligence (AI)

Typed requests are interpreted in your browser. Clear commands (places, agents, evidence) use deterministic rules. If you enable on-device AI, a small open model (Qwen3) runs in your browser with WebGPU; your requests are not sent to CORE or anyone else. Answers come from CORE’s recorded data and can be wrong: sources and limits are shown with them.

Voice input (optional)

Voice input and basic chat never require Qwen. Press the microphone to start; press it again or Escape to cancel. Already-available on-device recognition is preferred. Otherwise, a separate confirmation explains that your browser may send audio to its provider under that provider’s privacy policy. CORE does not receive, upload or store audio. Read answer uses a browser or system voice only when you request it; voice processing may be provider-managed. Transcripts use the same pipeline as typed requests. No speech pack is installed automatically. Support and connectivity vary; if recognition is unavailable, denied or fails, typing still works.

Data processed by the agents

Only public datasets listed in CORE’s data policy (for example DataSF, USGS, OpenStreetMap). Individual records such as 311 requests or names of permit holders are excluded by design.

Retention, deletion and contact

CORE keeps nothing about you on a server, so there is nothing to request from us: erase your data in “My CORE” or in your browser settings. Questions: github.com/vincentleg/core.

  • 无账户、无登录，也不会把表单发送到 CORE 服务器：CORE 没有面向访客的应用服务器或数据库。

  • CORE 不会用您输入的内容训练任何模型。
