INITIAL INSTITUTIONAL FOCUS · PROPOSED

CORE City: one local problem, a traceable path to review.

A municipal mobility team needs to assess a congested corridor, understand the evidence and hear residents before choosing an intervention. CORE’s existing San Francisco mobility dossier is a research demonstration of this journey, not a city deployment or customer pilot.

  8. Decision trackingLocal log; institutional workflow planned

Potential buyer: a municipal mobility, planning or innovation team. Proposed paid value: organize evidence review, consultations and project reporting in one accountable workspace. Demand, procurement fit and willingness to pay remain unvalidated.

Today: geographic evidence, recorded agent reasoning, uncalibrated traffic scenarios, local proposals and a mission log. Planned: shared municipal workspaces, resident consultations, assigned reviewers and institutional decision tracking. No real-world improvement is established.

Who it serves — and why they would pay

Individuals

Useful local knowledge gets lost; it is hard to see which sources support an idea.

Explore sourced problems, follow a mission and save an observation. CORE World stays free; Plus would offer personal organization and convenience.

Available now
Local profile, followed mission, private contributions and an encrypted Idea Vault.
Planned
Advanced collections, personalization, alerts and personal exports.

Businesses

Teams struggle to connect place-specific risks, evidence and proposed projects.

A project or operations team would pay for shared coordination, reporting and integrations rather than rebuilding the research trail in separate tools.

Available now
The same public research preview: sourced dossiers, local notes and mission reports. No team workspace.
Planned
Team projects, organizational dashboards, reporting and integrations.

Regions

Infrastructure and environmental questions cross municipal boundaries.

Regional planning teams would pay to coordinate scenarios and evidence across municipalities, with traceable review responsibilities.

Available now
A single-corridor research preview; no regional deployment or validated regional analysis.
Planned
Cross-municipal coordination, regional scenarios, infrastructure and environmental analysis, governance workflows.

Governments

National institutions need accountable analysis across agencies and jurisdictions.

Institutional program teams would pay for specialized deployments, integrations, security and auditable human oversight.

Available now
Public research tools and local evidence logs only; no institutional security certification or government adoption claimed.
Planned
National-scale workflows, specialized deployments, security controls and auditability.

A focused starting point. A wider opportunity.

The proposed business starts with one municipal workflow, then expands to more departments, municipalities and regional coordination. Recurring professional software and deployment services are the revenue hypothesis; CORE World provides free public access. The market spans organizations that make place-based decisions, but market size, customer demand, revenue and repeatability have not yet been established.

An integrated journey in an established market.

Esri / ArcGIS Urban supports geographic scenario planning; CivicPlus provides civic software; ClimateView supports climate transition planning. CORE’s proposed distinction is to connect geography, evidence, human contributions, AI-assisted reasoning, proposals, review and transparent progress in one journey. Integration is the product thesis, not a claim of superiority or exclusive capability.

Why CORE is being built

The founder brings experience in AI infrastructure, hardware, energy efficiency and business development. CORE applies that perspective to a practical question: how can technical evidence become understandable, reviewable work that people can contribute to?
