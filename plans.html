01 · THE PUBLIC FOUNDATION

CORE Free

$0

Understand the world. Take part in improving it.

CORE World is the open civilizational platform: a place to explore Earth, discover real-world questions and follow the evidence behind an investigation. You can inspect what an agent actually did, learn from public research and contribute an idea without buying influence.

Today, move from a mapped problem to its sources, a recorded investigation and an exploratory calculation. Save a contribution or follow the City mission in this browser. This is a research prototype; it is not yet a shared public discussion network.

Free participation is the foundation of every professional application. A subscription must never buy extra votes, preferential public proposal evaluation or privileged democratic influence.

AVAILABLE NOW
3D Earth, sourced dossiers, public knowledge views, recorded agent research, editable exploratory scenarios and browser-local contribution history. No account or cross-device continuity.
NEXT · PLANNED
Shared evidence and review workflows, public discussion and transparent contribution progress, as secure accounts and moderation become available. Equal civic rights remain free.
LONG-TERM VISION
An accessible living model of Earth, connecting public learning, human contributions and collective intelligence to reviewed, measurable progress.

02 · PERSONAL RESEARCH CONTINUITY

CORE Plus

$12/month · proposed

Make research cumulative, instead of starting over.

Plus is intended for people who repeatedly investigate a place, a problem or a field of knowledge. The reason to pay $12 each month would be continuity: an organized personal workspace that turns scattered reading into useful research you can return to.

Imagine following water resilience in your region: collect problems, keep private notes, compare saved scenarios and receive a digest of changes to the evidence. Export a personal report, revisit your research history and build a learning pathway around the questions you care about.

Plus would fund personal tools, organization, productivity and advanced assistance. It would not increase civic influence. Its value must be validated through sustained usefulness; none of these proposed paid tools can be activated today.

AVAILABLE NOW
The same free prototype as everyone else, including local contributions and mission following. No Plus subscription, private cloud workspace or personalized digest exists.
NEXT · PLANNED
Personal research workspace, organized saved problems, private collections and notes, place/topic following, research digests, saved-scenario comparison, exportable reports and research history. Limits and terms require validation.
LONG-TERM VISION
Guided learning, private research rooms and collaborative learning spaces; structured specialist-agent assistance only with an available, funded inference service. No unlimited agent conversations or compute is promised.

03 · OPERATIONAL & ECONOMIC INTELLIGENCE

CORE Business

From $299/month · proposed

Document a better operational decision.

For SMEs, industrial companies, logistics operators, site planners and sustainability teams, a location decision links many dependencies: energy, water, infrastructure, transportation, environmental exposure and operational risk. Evidence often lives across disconnected reports and departments.

A manufacturer considering two factory sites could use a future professional workspace to compare authorized energy and water records, transport access and infrastructure dependencies. A team would investigate uncertainties together, test supported assumptions and produce a reviewable investment report. This is a proposed workflow, not a working factory optimizer.

Businesses would pay for better geographic intelligence and coordinated decisions. Reduced research hours, avoided exposure and better resource planning are ROI hypotheses to measure with customers, not savings CORE has demonstrated. Unlike Plus, the focus is an organizational investment or operating decision.

AVAILABLE NOW
The free geographic research prototype, inspectable local-model investigation and exploratory transport calculation. No commercial site-selection model, business data integration or customer workspace is delivered.
NEXT · PLANNED
Organization-specific research spaces, authorized data integrations, shared investigations, comparable scenarios and decision reports. Start with a narrow customer decision and measure research time and decision quality.
LONG-TERM VISION
Validated cross-domain operational models and investment comparisons across sites, supply chains and resource dependencies, with explicit uncertainty and human authorization.

04 · MUNICIPAL SYSTEMS & PUBLIC REVIEW

CORE City

From $2,500/month · proposed

Turn fragmented evidence into reviewable choices.

For municipalities, planning departments, mobility agencies and infrastructure teams, an intervention is rarely isolated. Transportation, housing, energy, water, climate exposure and capital investment compete for attention across departments. Residents also need to understand the basis for a decision.

The San Francisco transport prototype demonstrates a narrow first step: sourced geography, a real recorded seven-agent investigation, shared evidence and an editable approach-queue calculation. A municipal team could eventually compare three district interventions, inspect trade-offs, gather public contributions and document a human decision. The current model is uncalibrated and cannot establish safety benefits or real traffic improvements.

City’s proposed value is faster evidence collection, less fragmentation and more structured comparison, with transparent reports and public participation. That operational value and willingness to pay are not yet validated. Unlike Business, City must serve public review and equal participation alongside internal coordination.

AVAILABLE NOW
The public SF mobility dossier, seven-agent recorded investigation, ten-task browser calculation and reproducible exploratory reports. No municipal deployment, validated digital twin or measured intervention outcome.
NEXT · PLANNED
A focused municipal pilot: secure team evidence workflows, citywide issue discovery, calibrated inputs where available, scenario review and public contribution processes with human decision gates.
LONG-TERM VISION
An interconnected operational city model spanning infrastructure and services, investment prioritization and outcome tracking supported by measured evidence.

05 · SYSTEMS THAT CROSS CITY BORDERS

CORE Region

From $10,000/month · proposed

Coordinate the decisions no city can solve alone.

Regional authorities, metropolitan planning organizations and intercity infrastructure agencies work with systems that cross municipal boundaries: transport corridors, electricity networks, watersheds, industrial logistics and climate exposure. Optimizing each city separately can move costs and risks onto its neighbors.

The Bay Area illustrates the need. A future regional workspace could compare infrastructure strategies across Oakland, San Francisco and San Jose, recording assumptions about energy demand, water availability, land use and transport. Shared evidence and cross-border scenarios would help agencies review resource and investment trade-offs together.

Region is proposed for multi-city coordination and interdependent resource planning, rather than a larger City dashboard. Its economic value would depend on reducing duplicated research and revealing conflicting investments; comprehensive regional simulation and these benefits remain unproven.

AVAILABLE NOW
500 geographic identities assigned across 16 Bay Area municipalities, verified boundaries and limited mapped pedestrian windows. Quantitative exploration remains restricted to supported SF approaches; this is not a comprehensive regional model.
NEXT · PLANNED
Shared regional planning workspaces, authorized multi-agency data, coordinated investigations and focused cross-city scenario pilots. Coverage and input quality would be explicit for each system.
LONG-TERM VISION
Interconnected transportation, energy, watersheds, resilience and land-use models, supporting reviewable cross-border investment and resource scenarios.

06 · NATIONAL SYSTEMS & ACCOUNTABILITY

CORE Government

Custom pricing · proposed

Bring cross-agency evidence into long-term planning.

National administrations, ministries and strategic public institutions must understand infrastructure, energy security, critical resources, climate resilience and industrial dependencies over long time horizons. Evidence provenance and the authority to act matter as much as analytical capability.

A future Government application could bring agencies together to compare national energy and infrastructure investment scenarios, preserving assumptions, uncertainty, expert review and audit records. Permissions, specialized integrations and secure or sovereign deployment requirements would need to be established for each institution before use.

The proposed commercial value lies in integrated analysis, governance and long-term scenario planning across agencies. Unlike Region, this addresses national system dependencies and institutional controls. CORE assists human decision-makers; it does not govern, authorize interventions or replace democratic accountability.

AVAILABLE NOW
The public research prototype only. No national integrations, sovereign deployments, certified security systems or validated national-scale simulations exist.
NEXT · PLANNED
Validate a bounded institutional use case and its permissions, evidence, auditability and deployment requirements. Any national operational offer would require further implementation and assurance.
LONG-TERM VISION
Cross-agency research and scenario environments with authorized data, strong governance, secure deployment options, auditable decisions and human authorization. National modeling remains an ambition, not a shipped service.
