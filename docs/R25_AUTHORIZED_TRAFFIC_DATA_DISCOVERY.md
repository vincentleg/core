# R25 — Authorized traffic data discovery (2026-10-23)

**NO-GO for calibrating CORE's current San Francisco street model.** Three official candidates were checked through catalog, license, and schema pages only. No measurement file or row was downloaded or ingested. `src/urban/model.mjs` requires directional motor-vehicle arrivals at one mapped approach in vehicles/hour; `data/urban/catalog.json` maps Folsom/Howard local-street centerlines, not state highways.

| Candidate, publisher, exact source | Observed variable; dates; geographic precision | Access versus reuse; fit |
|---|---|---|
| [SFMTA Corridor Counts 2014–2022](https://www.sfmta.com/TrafficCounts), [official CSV link](https://www.sfmta.com/media/40635/download?inline=), SFMTA | Prior `docs/pilot/source-manifest.json` metadata identifies directional block counts with `VOLUME_ADT`, `VOLUME_AM_PEAK`, `VOLUME_PM_PEAK`; Folsom Beale–Main, CNN 5652000, 13–14 Dec 2017. Peak units/method remain unverified. This block is outside CORE's Folsom 2nd–11th geometry. | **Publicly accessible; reuse unverified.** [SFMTA terms](https://www.sfmta.com/terms-of-use) provide no count-specific reuse grant. Ask SFMTA who owns the records and whether research analysis and publication are authorized, including vendor rights. |
| [DataSF Automated Speed Enforcement Citations](https://data.sf.gov/d/d5uh-bk84), City of San Francisco/SFMTA | Issued warnings and citations **per day, camera site and direction**, plus average speed for cited vehicles; first published 16 May 2025, updated 25 Aug 2026. Exact observation-date span and match to CORE approaches are unverified from metadata. **Citations are not passing-vehicle counts.** | **Public and explicitly reusable:** the [official federal harvest of DataSF metadata](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/automated-speed-enforcement-citations) names [ODC-PDDL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/pddl/1-0/). The source warns that even speeding vehicles are incompletely represented. Legally open, scientifically unsuitable for demand calibration. |
| [Caltrans Annual Average Daily Traffic](https://publish.data.ca.gov/dataset/annual-average-daily-traffic), Caltrans | Sampled vehicle counts adjusted into **AADT, vehicles/day**, at geocoded state-highway route/postmile points; [2024 layer metadata](https://caltrans-gis.dot.ca.gov/arcgis/rest/services/CHhighway/Traffic_AADT/FeatureServer/0/iteminfo) and [schema](https://caltrans-gis.dot.ca.gov/arcgis/rest/services/CHhighway/Traffic_AADT/FeatureServer/0) show point sites, route/postmile and back/ahead AADT/peak fields. Count years run Oct–Sep; exact sample dates are not provided on these pages. AADT is an estimate, not a dated hourly observation. | **Explicit catalog reuse statement:** [state dataset page](https://publish.data.ca.gov/dataset/annual-average-daily-traffic) labels it “Creative Commons Attribution” and says “No restrictions on public use”; attribution is required, but the version is unspecified. The [underlying layer terms](https://caltrans-gis.dot.ca.gov/arcgis/rest/services/CHhighway/Traffic_AADT/FeatureServer/0/iteminfo) say “solely for informational purposes”; reconcile that scope with the catalog grant before scientific use. [Caltrans confirms](https://dot.ca.gov/programs/traffic-operations/census) these counts cover state highways only, not CORE's local streets. |

**Decision and next evidence:** No reviewed source has both proven rights for the intended analysis and model-compatible, dated approach arrivals. The licensed DataSF measure counts enforcement actions; the licensed Caltrans measure covers highways and an annual estimate; the closest SFMTA count lacks a demonstrated reuse grant. Before a future experiment, obtain written SFMTA/vendor rights covering access, analysis and publication; identify same-epoch directional approach counts in vehicles/hour on a CORE segment across multiple comparable days; confirm lane and signal configuration, set aside an independent holdout, and arrange independent review. Remain exploratory, uncalibrated, and not field-validated.
