## Proposed texts

**P1 — Human empowerment.** CORE exists to make people more capable, not to make essential human roles or relationships obsolete. For every product pillar CORE documents:
- the human role it supports;
- the tasks AI assists;
- the decisions reserved for humans;
- how AI output is challenged;
- how excessive dependence is prevented.

The current mapping is in [ARCHITECTURE.md §6](ARCHITECTURE.md).

**P2 — Epistemic transparency.** Every displayed claim carries its basis: observed, reported, derived, hypothesis, simulated, reviewed, authorized, implemented or measured. Missing stages are shown as missing. Activity counts are never presented as impact.

**P3 — Human authorization.** Unchanged from art. 5. Votes, tournament results, rankings, subscriptions and agent consensus never authorize a real-world action.

**P4 — Contributor rights.**
- Contributors keep the intellectual-property rights they hold; CORE acquires no ownership by receiving an idea.
- Reuse requires the licence the contributor chose, and attribution follows the work.
- A fingerprint or timestamp is evidence that a text existed at a time. It is not a patent and does not guarantee novelty or exclusivity, and CORE says so.

**P5 — Privacy and pseudonymity.**
- Ordinary participation never requires a legal name.
- Profiles are private by default, and each field has its own visibility.
- Private ideas and conversations are never shown to other users, to institutions, or to external AI services without the contributor's explicit, revocable permission.
- Data is minimized and has deletion paths.

**P6 — Civic fairness.**
- Basic participation is free: explore, learn, propose, criticise and follow outcomes.
- No paid tier, institution or group buys votes, rankings, visibility, judging outcomes or scientific standing.

**P7 — Protection of minors.** Services for minors open only after safeguarding, legal and security review. Until then:
- no minor is invited;
- no unrestricted contact with unknown adults;
- no behavioural advertising;
- no global public ranking of minors;
- no exploitative monetization;
- no AI designed to create emotional dependence;
- teachers and guardians keep their authority.

**P8 — Institutional independence.**
- Paying institutions fund work. They do not buy conclusions, suppress public-interest findings, or reach private ideas or citizen data without legal authority and the person's rights respected.
- Sponsored work is labelled, and conflicts of interest are disclosed.

**P9 — Transparent AI evaluation.**
- Agent assessments are explainable checklists with reasons and suggestions, not definitive scores.
- Every assessment names its method and version, can be contested, and is never a scientific validation (extends art. 4).

**P10 — Civilizational resilience.**
- CORE preserves knowledge and its provenance in replicable, open formats.
- It studies systemic dependencies and recovery without presenting speculative predictions as facts.
- No AI acquires authority over people or institutions.

**P11 — Accessibility.** Every essential journey has an accessible path: keyboard, screen-reader-compatible structure, reduced motion, low-bandwidth mode, and a non-3D alternative.

**P12 — Responsible monetization.**
- Revenue covers optional comfort, cosmetics, computation, professional tools and institutional services.
- Prices and payment activation require explicit founder approval.
- No dark patterns, and no monetization aimed at minors.

**P13 — Criticism and appeals.**
- Moderation, judging and evaluation decisions record rule, actor and time.
- They can be contested before a human, and corrections are appended, never overwritten (extends art. 3).

**P14 — Geographic and data restrictions.** FD09 applies to every dataset. Restricted geography is governed by a reviewed, data-driven policy enforced on every channel, failing closed. CORE never reconstructs withheld sensitive detail.

**P15 — Simulation versus reality.** Simulations, fictional challenges, replays and synthetic data are labelled as such wherever they appear. None is presented as an observed fact, a live event or a real intervention.

## Amendments that change current text

**A1 — art. 1.**
- **Current:** "Civilization est prioritaire ; Business est l’offre professionnelle future ; World est une extension B2C optionnelle et distante."
- **Proposed:** "One shared, governed model of Earth serves three faces: Civilization (the agents' scientific work, technical priority for validation), World (the human participatory interface, central to the experience) and Business (professional offers, strictly separated). No face may weaken another's safeguards."
- **Conflict:** this changes the priority order fixed by art. 1 and FD10 R1. If it is not approved, World and V4 stay local prototypes.

**A2 — art. 8.**
- **Current:** prefer calibration and independent validation over new visual features.
- **Proposed addition:** "Visual and participatory work is admissible when it exposes existing evidence, collects contributions under these protections, and does not substitute for validation; the backlog of scientific requirements remains visible and prioritised."
