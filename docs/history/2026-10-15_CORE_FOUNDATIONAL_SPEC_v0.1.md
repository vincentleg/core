# CORE — Creation Of Reborn Earth
## Foundational Constitution & Master Product Specification
**Version:** 0.1 — Historical reconstruction  
**Date:** October 15, 2026  
**Status:** Draft for founder review, not a final ratified constitution

---

## 1. Founding vision

CORE is both a technology company and a long-term participatory civilizational project.

Its purpose is to help humanity understand complex systems, identify important problems, explore solutions, test hypotheses, and make measurable progress.

CORE must combine scientific reasoning, artificial intelligence, simulation, human knowledge, collective participation, and responsible governance.

CORE is not simply a chatbot, an agent framework, a dashboard, or a virtual world.

It is intended to become an evolving scientific and civilizational intelligence platform.

**Foundational principle:** CORE must never confuse simulated progress, predictions, proposals, or demonstrations with verified real-world achievements.

## 2. Two distinct but connected systems

### CORE Business

The professional and enterprise-facing platform.

Its intended purpose is to help organizations understand, simulate, optimize, and improve complex systems, particularly infrastructure, energy, water, computing, and other measurable domains.

The initial business opportunity includes AI and data-center infrastructure efficiency.

The architecture must allow additional domains without claiming unsupported expertise or results.

Potential capabilities include:
- Data analysis and scientific diagnostics.
- Hypothesis generation and comparison.
- Infrastructure simulation.
- Resource-efficiency optimization.
- Risk and uncertainty assessment.
- Evidence-based recommendations.
- Measured-outcome tracking.
- Enterprise-specific privacy and authorization controls.

**Not yet ratified:** final customer segments, pricing, contractual responsibility, hosting model, and commercial data-processing boundaries.

### CORE World

The accessible, participatory civilizational platform.

Its purpose is to allow people to explore problems affecting humanity, understand proposed solutions, contribute knowledge, and participate in collective progress.

CORE World must not become merely a decorative public interface for CORE Business.

It requires its own participation, transparency, privacy, and governance architecture.

Both systems can share authorized scientific knowledge and infrastructure, but private enterprise data must never become public without appropriate authorization.

## 3. Participatory virtual Earth

CORE World includes the long-term vision of an explorable virtual Earth.

Participants should be able to:
- Explore geographical areas and civilizational challenges.
- Discover proposals and their evidence.
- See leading hypotheses and their limitations.
- Contribute ideas, observations, evidence, or critiques.
- Follow how contributions are evaluated.
- Understand which proposals are accepted, rejected, revised, or awaiting review.
- See verified outcomes where real measurements exist.

The virtual Earth must distinguish:
1. Real-world observations.
2. Scientific models.
3. Simulated scenarios.
4. Unverified proposals.
5. Measured real-world impacts.

A visual representation must never imply that a real-world change has occurred when only a simulation exists.

## 4. Human identities, avatars and contributions

Participants may create an optional personal identity or avatar.

The avatar can evolve through verified participation and contributions, but should not reward unverified claims, manipulation, or mere activity volume.

A participant's history may include:
- Ideas submitted.
- Evidence contributed.
- Reviews performed.
- Revisions and critiques.
- Decisions affecting their proposals.
- Authorized reuse of their contributions.
- Verified impacts, where measured.

Participation must protect privacy, consent, contributor rights, and appropriate correction or withdrawal mechanisms.

Precise location disclosure must be optional and appropriately protected.

**Not yet ratified:** identity assurance levels, avatar rendering technology, detailed reputation mechanics, retention and deletion policy, and anti-Sybil implementation.

## 5. Autonomous civilization — Phase 9 foundational vision

CORE's autonomous civilization must be more than a collection of static agent profiles or animated characters.

Its intended foundation includes:
- Persistent individual agent identities.
- Distinct professions and responsibilities.
- Organizational hierarchies.
- Persistent memory.
- Learning and adaptation.
- Missions, goals and decisions.
- Relationships and interactions.
- Movement constrained by the represented geography and streets.
- Autonomous activity.
- Observable consequences of agent decisions.

A genuine autonomous civilization requires a persistent simulated world state that agents can perceive and change within explicitly defined permissions.

Agent movement must respect the applicable spatial model rather than allowing arbitrary teleportation.

A displayed profession must not imply that the agent possesses the corresponding executable capability.

A scripted demonstration must not be described as genuine autonomous behavior.

### Still requiring founder specification

The following are not established in sufficient detail:
- Agent needs and daily routines.
- Economic and resource systems.
- Social relationships and institutions.
- Learning objectives and mechanisms.
- Exact world-state variables.
- Autonomous scheduling and operating budget.
- Whether and how agents can propose actions affecting real infrastructure.

Until ratified, these elements must remain requirements under specification, not invented implementations.

## 6. Scientific engine

CORE's scientific intelligence must be capable of:
- Formulating explicit hypotheses.
- Comparing competing explanations.
- Assessing available evidence.
- Identifying missing evidence.
- Recording assumptions.
- Estimating uncertainty.
- Performing sensitivity analysis.
- Using authorized simulations when scientifically justified.
- Tracking provenance and reproducibility.
- Submitting conclusions for appropriate human review.

Agent-generated conclusions are proposals, not automatically established scientific facts.

Every scientific claim must preserve its epistemic status.

## 7. Scientific memory and provenance

CORE must maintain a traceable record of:
- Evidence sources.
- Dataset versions.
- Hypotheses.
- Model assumptions.
- Agent-generated proposals.
- Human reviews.
- Rejections.
- Revisions.
- Supersessions.
- Conflicts.
- Validated results.

Human-approved findings must not be silently replaced by agent-generated findings.

Rewording a rejected proposal must not provide a way to bypass human review.

The current word-overlap detection is an initial safeguard, not a complete semantic protection.

## 8. Human authority and governance

CORE's agents may analyze, propose, simulate and critique within their authorized scope.

Consequential decisions require appropriate human authorization.

The governance system must support:
- Clear authority boundaries.
- Human review.
- Emergency stops.
- Auditable decisions.
- Independent scientific review where necessary.
- Protection against manipulation.
- Conflict-of-interest handling.
- Contributor rights.
- Transparent proposal histories.
- Reversible actions where technically possible.

Public participation does not automatically confer permission to modify scientific truth, production infrastructure, or real-world systems.

## 9. Data rights — FD09

CORE must only access and use data when the intended access and use are demonstrably authorized.

Public accessibility is not proof of unrestricted reuse rights.

Before external data operations, CORE must verify:
- Official source.
- Applicable access conditions.
- Licensing and permitted uses.
- Scientific and commercial reuse permissions where relevant.
- Attribution obligations.
- Privacy restrictions.
- Redistribution and external processing restrictions.

CORE must never bypass authentication, paywalls, technical restrictions, rate limits, or licensing conditions.

If authorization is unclear, the affected operation must remain blocked.

The NYC traffic pilot remains blocked under G0 until its applicable official conditions are verified and the founder authorizes proceeding.

## 10. Simulation and real-world impact

CORE must maintain a strict separation between:
- Simulated improvement.
- Predicted improvement.
- Laboratory or benchmark result.
- Independently reviewed result.
- Measured real-world improvement.

No real-world impact may be claimed without adequate measurement and provenance.

CORE should track multidimensional civilizational progress rather than reducing progress to a single score.

The Kardashev scale may represent an energy-related dimension, but must not serve as the sole definition of human progress.

**Still requiring founder specification:** the exact multidimensional impact framework, metrics, measurement partners, and success criteria.

## 11. Empirical validation — Phase 14

Scientific validation requires:
- Authorized datasets.
- A documented scientific question.
- A preregistered experimental protocol.
- Baselines.
- Clearly separated calibration, validation, and untouched holdout data.
- Defined metrics and acceptance criteria.
- Independent review where required.
- Reproducible results.
- Publication of negative or inconclusive outcomes.

Previously inspected observations cannot be treated as untouched holdout evidence.

The NYC preregistration remains a draft and is not sealed.

No empirical validation claim is permitted until the required procedure has actually been completed.

## 12. Engineering and security

CORE must be developed with:
- Deterministic and reproducible tests where feasible.
- Data provenance.
- Secure authorization boundaries.
- Secrets protection.
- Explicit external-action permissions.
- Cost controls.
- Regression testing.
- Auditable changes.
- Clear distinction between local, experimental, and production behavior.

External services, paid resources, deployments, merges, and consequential production changes remain subject to founder authorization.

## 13. Current implementation status

The October 15 foundational audit reports 44 requirements:

- 14 verified complete within their tested scope.
- 11 partially implemented.
- 6 prototypes.
- 3 documented only.
- 6 missing.
- 4 blocked.

Current strengths include persistent agent identities, memory, provenance, scientific gates, bounded movement, and integrity checks.

Major unfinished areas include autonomous daily behavior, agent-driven world-state changes, complete participatory systems, authenticated human decisions, empirical validation, and broader scientific simulation.

These classifications are audit findings, not proof that the complete founding vision has been delivered.

## 14. Development acceptance principle

A feature is complete only when:
1. Its intended behavior is precisely specified.
2. The implementation exists.
3. The implementation is exercised by appropriate tests.
4. The behavior is demonstrated.
5. Security and governance constraints are satisfied.
6. Scientific claims are supported by the required evidence.
7. Remaining limitations are disclosed.

Documentation alone is not implementation.

A prototype is not a finished capability.

A passing test suite is not proof of completeness of the entire vision.

## 16. Non-negotiable founding direction

CORE must remain a platform where scientific intelligence, human participation, and responsible action reinforce one another.

Its long-term ambition is to help humanity understand its challenges and contribute to solutions across generations.

Its credibility must come from evidence, transparent reasoning, reproducibility, accountable governance, and measurable outcomes — never from invented achievements.
