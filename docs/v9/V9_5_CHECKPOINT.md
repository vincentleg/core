- Model: deterministic one-second fluid point queue at a single mapped approach, uniform assumed arrivals and periodic effective-green service. Editable baseline plus two alternatives (core supports 2–4). Vehicle conservation, accrued delay, queue, remaining vehicles, capacity/throughput and capped demand sensitivity are calculated. All traffic inputs are assumptions; geometry-derived length is the sourced context. Residual queues are censored; assumed storage is checked but no spillback, turns, transit, pedestrian effects or validated forecast is modeled. Different-demand comparisons and ties can be inconclusive; best scenario is computed, never prescribed.
