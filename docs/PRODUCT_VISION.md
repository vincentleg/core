# Product vision

CORE — Creation Of Reborn Earth — aims to make infrastructure decisions testable before they affect people. A parallel Earth provides context; the central product is the evidence and human decision process connecting a proposal to implementation.

The intended first customer problem is coordination of road and utility works: evaluate joint schedules and closures so cities and operators can avoid reopening the same street unnecessarily. Traffic signals are the current visual demonstration, not evidence that a roadworks coordination product already exists.

The intended workflow is: define a bounded problem, identify dated evidence, calibrate an appropriate simulator, generate alternatives, challenge them with adverse scenarios, and present a conditional permit proposal to a qualified human. After separately authorized implementation, observed outcomes would support recalibration. Metrics should include measured disruption, coordination time and repeat excavation; approval rate is not a success metric.

The current prototype supplies synthetic traffic experiments and a deterministic permit demonstration. It does not supply a real-time planetary replica, calibrated outcomes, actual AI reasoning, signatures, customer workflows or real-world execution.

Long-term directions include buildings and energy, water and utility networks, subsurface context and spatial interfaces. Planetary coordination and expansion beyond Earth are aspirations, not engineering commitments or validated market claims. Each new domain needs its own data provenance, simulation validity and accountable decision process.

Non-negotiable boundaries: agents propose and simulate; deterministic rules govern proposal eligibility; humans authorize. No synthetic data is described as observed, no uncalibrated gain as a real improvement, and no unknown evidence as proof of safety. Sensitive infrastructure information must not enter this public prototype.
